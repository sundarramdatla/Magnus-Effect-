# The Magnus Effect 

The Magnus Effect is a well understood phenomeon occuring in projectules subject to some kind of spin, such as a top spin on a ping pong ball. The Magnus force acts normally to both the velocity and angular velocity of the projectile, resulting in an often unexpected curving of its trajectory.
This MATLAB environment allows the user to experiment with the velocity and angular velocity vector of their choice to visualize the Magnus force with drag and other aerodynamic parameters.

Simulation uses MATLAB's ODE45 to time step kinematic equations of motions for a projectile with a Magnus and quadratic drag force term.
